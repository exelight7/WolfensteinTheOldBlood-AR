# Roadmap

1. Repository foundation and source inventory.
2. Verified archive reader and complete localization extraction.
3. Stable source IDs, source hashes, public Arabic database, local bilingual reference, placeholder QA.
4. Determine font serialization and obtain licensed IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
5. Prove rebuild and one Arabic menu string in-game.
6. Test Arabic shaping, RTL, mixed Latin, numbers, icons and wrapping.
7. Review a small UI/subtitle pilot, then one chapter.
8. Translate remaining UI, story and supplemental content in resumable batches.
9. Full-game visual QA and supported-version registration.
10. Safe installer/uninstaller and reproducible patch-only release.

Mass translation is gated on successful extraction, reference generation, placeholder validation, rebuild and in-game Arabic rendering.
